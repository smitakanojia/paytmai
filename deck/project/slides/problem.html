The problem

Support is measured on deflection. The clock is measured in rupees.

13.34 L

complaints reached the RBI Ombudsman in FY25, up 13.5% year on year.

Mobile and electronic banking accounted for 16.86% of them. Every one of those began as a deadline somebody missed.

“Resolved” can mean the merchant gave up

Deflection rates count silence as success. No deflection metric asks whether the money actually moved.

Klarna optimised exactly this

~2.3M chats in month one, company-equated to ~700 agents. By May 2025 its CEO said cost had become too dominant and quality fell; Klarna rehired humans. Deflection-optimised AI failed — not AI.

Compensation that is owed goes unpaid

RBI intends the ₹100/day credit to be automatic. In practice it waits for a human to notice. Nobody counts the leak.

Sources: RBI Annual Report of the Ombudsman Scheme FY2024-25 · Forbes, May 2025

Lead with the 13.34 lakh number — it is the scale of failure that has already escaped the industry's own support systems and landed on the regulator's desk, and it grew 13.5 percent in a year. Then the three failure modes. On Klarna, be precise and fair: the lesson is not that AI support does not work, it is that optimising for deflection does not work. That distinction is what our metric is built to avoid.
