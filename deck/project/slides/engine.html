The solution

T+0 attaches a clock to every stuck rupee and drives it to zero.

01 · ATTACH

From telemetry, not the complaint

A failed or delayed transaction opens a case with all four deadlines computed — whether or not the merchant has noticed.

02 · RANK

Money at risk × time to breach

The worklist is sorted by what the next hour costs, not by how old the ticket is.

03 · ACT

Inside the policy gate

Chase the settlement, raise the UDIR entry inside the cycle, push the reversal, credit what is owed — under coded caps.

04 · PROVE

One acceptable close state

Money moved, clock stopped, and compensation paid if owed — verified in the backend, never inferred from silence.

Nothing that moves money bypasses the policy gate.

Nothing closes without proof that the clock actually stopped.

Walk the four stages left to right. The one to dwell on is ATTACH: the case exists before the complaint does, which is what makes everything after it possible. Then the two invariants at the bottom — these are the guardrails, and they are the answer to the safety question before anyone asks it.
