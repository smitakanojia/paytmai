Problem statement · the four deadlines

In Indian payments, every deadline is written down — and every one of them has a price.

ClockRuleWhat a breach costs
Auto-reversalRBI TAT circular, UPI → T+1₹100 per day payable to the customer, automatically, with no claim filed
UDIR conversionNPCI, P2M unanswered 3 daysThe complaint converts itself into a chargeback
Settlement cycleNPCI, effective 15 Feb 2025Chargeback auto-decided on the TCC/RET raised in the next cycle — silence loses
Grievance windowRBI Integrated Ombudsman SchemeAt 30 days the case becomes the regulator’s, with awards against the entity

Every support product optimises deflection. None of them optimises the clock.
