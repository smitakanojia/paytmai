Our USP

Every other agent ends in a sentence. Ours ends in a credit.

RUNG 1

Inform

“Your ₹5,000 from 2:14 pm is held at the bank. Due back by 6 pm tomorrow.”

RUNG 2

Chase

Raises the UDIR entry inside the settlement cycle and pushes the reversal. No human in the path.

RUNG 3

Compensate

Computes the TAT credit at ₹100 a day and pays it, citing the clause. The merchant never asked.

RUNG 4

Bridge

If the supplier cannot wait, advances the settlement — a paid Paytm product, sold at the moment of need.

It pays merchants money they did not know they were owed, before they ask, because a regulator says they are owed it.

This is the slide to slow down on. Rungs one and two are table stakes dressed properly. Rung three is the one no deployed product does — we searched and found nothing that pays regulatory compensation unprompted. Rung four is the sales moment the track asks for, and it is not invented: Paytm already sells instant settlement and already runs delinquency models that can price the advance. Read the closing line slowly and then stop talking.
