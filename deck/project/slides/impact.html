Impact & benefits

Three parties gain, and each gain is a number we can check.

The merchant

  • Resolved in about one turn, because the agent opens with their transaction instead of a menu
  • Told during an outage even if they never complained
  • Paid compensation they had never heard of
  • Working capital unblocked when the supplier cannot wait

Paytm

  • Penalties, lost chargebacks and Ombudsman cases avoided — in rupees
  • Support load during an incident stays flat instead of rising with ticket count
  • Deflection replaced by a close state that can be audited
  • A regulator-readable trail behind every autonomous action

The rail

  • Compliance becomes the default path rather than an escalation
  • Every circular encoded once is reusable across every bank and partner
  • Fewer disputes reach the regulator at all — 13.34 lakh did in FY25
