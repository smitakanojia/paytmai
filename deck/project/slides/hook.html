Problem statement

2:14 pm. A merchant’s ₹5,000 does not arrive. Four clocks start at once.

T+0

Money is stuck

The merchant has not complained yet. No ticket exists.

T+1

Reversal is due

Past this, RBI’s TAT rule makes ₹100 per day payable — automatically, no claim needed.

Day 3

UDIR converts it

An unanswered P2M complaint turns itself into a chargeback.

Day 30

Ombudsman opens

The case leaves Paytm’s hands and enters the regulator’s.

The merchant knows none of this. Neither does the support queue — it sorts by ticket age.

Set the scene concretely. One merchant, one stuck payment, four separate legal deadlines running from the same instant. The point of the slide is the last line: every support system on earth prioritises by how old the ticket is, not by how many hours until a regulatory deadline costs real money.
