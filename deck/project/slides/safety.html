Proposed solution · safety architecture

An agent that moves money must be unable to invent a rule.

Moffatt v. Air Canada, 2024 BCCRT 149

The airline argued its chatbot was a separate legal entity responsible for its own words. The tribunal rejected that outright and held Air Canada responsible for everything on its site — static page or chatbot alike.

The bot had invented a refund policy. That is the failure mode we designed against.

Policy is codified regulation, not generated text

The rule file is a machine-readable encoding of published RBI and NPCI circulars. The model plans and writes; it has no authority to state a policy that is not in the file.

Every rupee traces to a tool call

Each amount, timestamp and deadline in a reply carries the tool result and the clause it came from, in an append-only log.

We report a measured error rate, not a claim of zero

Caps, fraud flags and human approval above ₹5,000 — plus a false-action rate with its confidence interval, measured on an adversarial set built to induce a wrongful payout.

Air Canada is the right case because the failure was not a hallucinated fact, it was an invented policy that the company was then held to. Our architecture makes that structurally impossible rather than unlikely. The third card matters more than it looks: every other team will put a zero on this slide. A measured number with a stated sample size and an adversarial set reads as far more rigorous to anyone who has shipped a model.
